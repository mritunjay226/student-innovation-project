1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROBLEM STATEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Problem:
1. Linear Curricula & Black-Box Scores: Traditional EdTech platforms treat learning as a linear conveyor belt. When a student fails a diagnostic quiz (e.g., scoring 60% on Calculus or Physics), the system simply flags a low score without identifying why or where the foundational misconception occurred.
2. Passive Video Consumption & Rote Memorization: Watching videos and guessing multiple-choice options does not build deep cognitive intuition. Students memorize surface-level steps without understanding the underlying mechanisms.
3. The Ebbinghaus Forgetting Curve: Without scientifically calibrated spaced review intervals, learners forget more than 80% of newly learned concepts within 48 hours.
4. Educator Blindspots: Teachers lack real-time visibility into shared class-wide misconceptions and prerequisite dependencies across their syllabus.

The Solution (Axiora):
Axiora is a dual-sided (Student Portal & Teacher Console) intelligent learning platform that transitions education from passive memorization to active cognitive mastery through:
- Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) Knowledge Engine: Pinpoints root-cause learning gaps by traversing prerequisite dependencies backwards.
- Multi-Agent Feynman "Teach-Back" Classroom Pod: Students reverse-tutor a pod of 4 AI classmates (Toby, Maya, Leo, Sam) with live comprehension meters and dynamic blackboard formulas.
- Multimodal Document-to-Flashcards Engine: Extracts high-yield cards from lecture slides/PDFs using Google Gemini Vision OCR paired with modified SM-2 / Leitner Spaced Repetition algorithms.
- Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Teacher Command Center: AI-assisted curriculum ingestion, visual graph verification, and real-time class learning gap signal matrices.


2. THE TWO-SIDED USER ECOSYSTEM & WORKFLOWS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Student Experience:
1. Dashboard & Knowledge Radar: Visualizes multi-dimensional concept mastery and retention risk across STEM subjects (Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry for Classes 10, 11, 12).
2. Diagnostic Quizzes: Adaptive assessments calibrated to the student's Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD), tracking response latency and self-rated confidence.
3. Feynman Teach-Back Studio: Active peer-tutoring where the student explains concepts to 4 AI classmates. The system measures pod comprehension (0-100%), provides dynamic blackboard tips, awards Tutor Ranks (e.g., Feynman Grandmaster), badges, and XP.
4. PDF Flashcards Studio: Converts uploaded documents/slides into 5 card formats (Direct Q&A, Explanatory, One-Word Recall, MCQ, Mixed), utilizing 3D card flips, procedural sound effects, and spaced review scheduling.

B. Teacher / Educator Experience:
1. Interactive Visual Prerequisite Map: Visual DAG powered by React Flow (@xyflow/react) where teachers can view, create, verify, or remove prerequisite links between concepts.
2. Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Ingestion: Upload lecture notes/slides; AI proposes extracted concepts, questions, and prerequisite edges into an approval queue for teacher verification.
3. Class Learning Gaps & Signals: Aggregates class performance to classify concepts into "Strong", "Watch", or "High Gap", recommending specific remediation interventions.
4. Student Roster: In-depth drilldown into individual student mastery, retention risks, and active misconceptions.


3. KEY FEATURES & DEEP DIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Feature 1: Multi-Agent Feynman Teach-Back Pod
- Pedagogical Foundation: The Feynman Technique ("To master a concept, explain it simply to someone else").
- The 4 AI Classmate Personas:
  * Toby (Visual & Intuitive Learner): Needs physical analogies (speedometer, balloons, water flow); confused by unexplained jargon.
  * Maya (Skeptical Challenger): Probes mathematical rigor, boundary conditions, and edge-case exceptions.
  * Leo (Quick Quizzer): Energetic, gives rapid pop-quizzes, bulleted takeaways, and celebrates combo streaks.
  * Sam (Direct & Precise): Provides direct, factual definitions, step-by-step equations, and zero fluff.
- Features: Real-time comprehension meters per classmate, dynamic LaTeX blackboard formulas, badge unlocks (Analogy Maestro, Skeptic Silencer, Combo Surge), and automated misconception detection saved to the database.

Feature 2: Knowledge DAG & Root-Cause Prerequisite Engine
- Structure: Concepts represented as graph vertices; pedagogical dependencies represented as directed edges (Prerequisite A -> Advanced Concept B).
- Root-Cause Isolation: When a student fails an advanced diagnostic item (e.g., Chain Rule), the engine traces upstream dependencies (Limits, Function Composition) to pinpoint the exact failure point.
- Dynamic Calibration: Adjusts difficulty in real-time based on student mastery and confidence scores.

Feature 3: Multimodal PDF-to-Flashcards & Spaced Repetition
- Multimodal Extraction: Powered by Google Gemini Vision/OCR for uploaded PDFs or text.
- 5 Card Style Presets:
  1. Direct Question (Concise Q&A)
  2. Explanatory Concept (Deep mechanisms & proofs)
  3. One-Word Recall (Fill-in-the-blank keyword recall)
  4. Multiple Choice Question (MCQ with distractor explanations)
  5. Mixed Deck
- Spaced Repetition Algorithm (SM-2 / Leitner):
  * R = e^(-t / S)
  * Again (<10 min reset) -> Hard (1 day) -> Good (2-14 days) -> Easy (4-30 days).
  * Counteracts the Ebbinghaus forgetting curve.

Feature 4: Teacher Suite & HITL Safety
- Slide Ingestion Pipeline: AI extracts curriculum nodes and proposes confidence-weighted edges.
- Human Verification: Teachers approve, edit, or reject AI proposals, ensuring zero black-box hallucinations enter the syllabus.
- Class Gap Matrix: Categorizes topics into Strong, Watch, and High Gap, enabling targeted classroom interventions.

5. UNIQUE SELLING PROPOSITIONS (USPs) FOR JUDGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Reverse-Tutoring over Passive Chatbots: Students teach the AI rather than receiving generated answers, fostering deep retention.
2. Root-Cause DAG Traversal: Isolates upstream missing prerequisites instead of assigning repetitive busywork.
3. Human-in-the-Loop Pedagogical Safety: Combines AI extraction speed with teacher verification.
4. Grounded in Cognitive Science: Incorporates Bloom's Taxonomy, Vygotsky's ZPD, Feynman Technique, and Ebbinghaus Spaced Repetition.
